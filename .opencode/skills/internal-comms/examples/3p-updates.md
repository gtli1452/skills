# 3P Updates

Use this format for short internal updates organized as **Progress, Plans, Problems**.

## Inputs to collect
- Team or project name
- Dates covered
- 2-5 measurable wins from the recent period
- 2-5 next-step priorities
- 1-3 real blockers, risks, or dependencies

## Draft shape
[Emoji] [Team Name] ([Dates Covered])
Progress: [1-3 concise sentences]
Plans: [1-3 concise sentences]
Problems: [1-3 concise sentences]

## Guidance
- Keep the entire update readable in 30-60 seconds.
- Use metrics when they improve clarity.
- Scale detail to the audience: broader audience means less task-level detail.
- If there are no material blockers, say so directly instead of leaving Problems empty.

## Validation
- Each section is brief and factual.
- Progress covers the past period; Plans covers the next period.
- Problems are actual blockers, not disguised status updates.
