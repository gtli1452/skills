# Company Newsletter

Use this format for a weekly or monthly company-wide roundup.

## Inputs to collect
- Official announcements and leadership notes
- Major milestones, launches, or operating changes
- Important links to docs, recordings, or reference material
- Cross-functional updates worth broad visibility

## Suggested structure
:megaphone: Company announcements
- [Top announcement]
- [Top announcement]

:dart: Progress on priorities
- [Area or program]
  - [Concrete update]
  - [Concrete update]

:pillar: Leadership updates
- [Leadership note or policy change]

:thread: Social or community highlights
- [Optional morale or culture update]

## Guidance
- Aim for roughly 12-25 bullets, depending on the amount of real news.
- Keep each bullet to 1-2 sentences.
- Prioritize company-wide relevance over team-only details.
- Write in a direct, shared-company voice when appropriate.

## Validation
- Sections are scannable.
- The biggest updates appear first.
- Links are attached where readers will want more detail.
- Duplicate or low-signal items have been removed.
