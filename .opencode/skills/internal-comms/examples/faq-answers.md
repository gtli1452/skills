# FAQ Answers

Use this format when recurring internal questions need short, trustworthy answers.

## Inputs to collect
- The actual questions people keep asking
- The best available official answer or owner-confirmed answer
- Known unknowns, timelines, or follow-up owners

## Draft shape
- **Question:** [One sentence]
- **Answer:** [1-3 sentences]
- **Source or owner:** [Optional but useful]

## Guidance
- Keep the question phrased the way employees would naturally ask it.
- Answer what is known first, then note uncertainty plainly.
- Do not invent policy, timelines, or decisions.
- If the answer depends on role, location, or team, say that explicitly.

## Validation
- Questions represent real confusion points.
- Answers are concise and sourced.
- Unknowns are called out instead of being smoothed over.
